## Taking part

You take part in a course through an **enrolment**: a teacher enrols you, you enrol yourself, or you are enrolled because you belong to a group of your campus. While your enrolment is active you see the course and do what your role in it allows.

An enrolment can have a start and an end. Before it starts and after it ends you do not take part, even if the enrolment is still there.

## Enrolling yourself

When a course lets you enrol yourself, it may ask for an **enrolment key** your teacher gave you. Some keys also put you in a group of the course. If the key is wrong, Kampus only says so, without hints; after five wrong keys in an hour you have to wait before trying again.

Some courses ask a teacher to **approve** you first: you send a request, optionally with a message, and you take part once it is approved. You can withdraw your request while it waits.

## When an enrolment ends

When your enrolment ends, the course decides what happens: it is suspended, removed, or you keep taking part without your role. Your grades and progress are never deleted when an enrolment ends: they stay with you in the course and come back if you are enrolled again.
