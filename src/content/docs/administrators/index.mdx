As an administrator or manager you shape the campus: categories and courses, roles and who holds them, enrolment methods, settings, the words and custom fields of your organisation, the layout and theme, rules that automate routine work, plugins, languages, privacy requests and background tasks. Every setting and customisation is resolved through the scope tree (campus, category, course, section), shows where its value comes from, and records who changed it.

## What you can do

**Designed** means the feature is specified and being built; **Available** gives the version that brought it.
