## Conversations

You write to people in conversations of three kinds:

- **With one person.** There is only one conversation for each pair of people, whoever opens it first, so everything you two say stays together.
- **With several people.** You give it a title and choose up to 49 people. Anyone in it can leave.
- **With a group of a course.** If you belong to a group, it has a conversation. People who join the group join the conversation, and people who leave the group leave it.

Your conversations are listed with the most recently active first, along with how many messages you have not read. Opening a conversation and reading it marks its messages read.

## Who you can write to

Your campus decides who can write to whom. Usually you can write to people who take part in one of your courses, but a campus may let everyone of it write to each other, or turn messages off. Administrators can write to anyone of the campus. If you are not allowed to write to someone, Kampus says so, but never why.

## Writing

Messages can have paragraphs, bold, italics, lists, quotes, code and links to web pages or email addresses. Anything else, such as scripts, images or embedded pages, is removed before your message is kept.

Each new message reaches the other people in your inbox and, if you allow it, as a push notification on your browsers and phones. **Mute** a conversation to stop being notified about it. You still see its messages.

## Deleting

Deleting a message removes it for you only. Once everyone in the conversation has deleted it, Kampus removes it for good. Your campus can also remove messages after a number of days.

## Blocking

Block a person so neither of you can write to the other, in your conversation or a new one. They are not told. You can unblock them whenever you like. A block does not stop you both writing in a group you share.
