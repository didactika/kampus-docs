Kampus is where you take your courses: you open their sections and activities, submit your work, see your grades and progress, talk with your teachers and classmates, and keep your calendar. Everything you see uses the words and the look your campus chose.

## What you can do

The list comes from the specification of Kampus. **Designed** means the feature is specified and being built; **Available** gives the version that brought it.

## Your data

You can export a copy of everything Kampus keeps about you, and ask for its deletion, from your profile. Your campus tells you its privacy notice and how long it keeps each kind of data.
