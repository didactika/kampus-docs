Available since 0.1.0.

## Asking for a copy

You can ask for an export of the personal data Kampus keeps about you: your grades and their history, what you have done in your courses, your notifications and your personal calendar entries. Each part of Kampus answers for its own data, so the request has one part per kind of data and is ready when every part is.

## Getting it

The export is ready a few minutes after you ask, and you can read it for seven days, as a single file you can download; after that Kampus forgets it, and you can ask again. Only you can read the export you asked for. If an administrator asks for your data on your behalf, they read that export, not you.

## Policies you accept

Your campus may publish policies, such as its terms of use, and ask you to accept them. You see the latest version of each one and which ones are waiting for you. When the campus publishes a new version you accept it again; accepting twice changes nothing. Which version you accepted, and when, is part of the export you can ask for.

## Asking to delete your data

You can also ask Kampus to delete your personal data, and you have to say why. An administrator reads the request and approves or rejects it; if you are an administrator yourself, your own request goes ahead at once. You can only have one deletion open at a time, and a rejected one does not stop you from asking again.

When it is approved, each part of Kampus erases what only concerns you, such as your notifications and your personal calendar entries. What belongs to the academic record, your grades and your progress, is kept for the time your campus decides, then deleted for good. It points to your account, which stays as an empty shell with a made-up name instead of yours. Your username, email, password and sessions are erased, and you cannot sign in again.

If your campus has a legal reason to keep your data, such as an open investigation, the request waits until that reason ends. You can see it waiting.
