Available since 0.1.0.

## What the calendar of a course shows

The calendar of a course lists, for the dates you choose, the entries your teachers added (an exam, a live session) and, for each activity you can see, when it **opens**, when it is **due**, when it **closes** and the last moment a late submission is accepted. If your teacher gave you an extension, the dates you see are yours, not those of everyone else.

## Your own entries

You can add entries only you see, such as when you plan to study. Nobody else, not even an administrator, can see or change them. Delete them at any time.

## What teachers and managers can do

People who manage the calendar of a course, a category or the campus add entries for everyone who sees it. A calendar shows up to a year at a time.
