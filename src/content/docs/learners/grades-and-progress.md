## Your grades

The page of a course shows your grades beside its content: each grade with its record, and the total of the course when the course has one. You see your own grades only, as soon as your teacher shows them. A grade your teacher hid, for example until every submission is marked, does not appear until the date they chose, and no other page shows it before then.

When a teacher changes one of your grades, you receive a notification. If you leave a course and are enrolled again, your grades are still there.

## Your progress

The same page shows how much of the course you have completed and the state of each activity: not started, in progress, done, passed or failed, depending on what the activity asks. Some activities complete by themselves when you view them or get a grade; others let you mark them done. While the grade a pass depends on is hidden, you see that the activity is done, but not whether you passed.

A course can also ask for other courses to be completed first; the page tells you which.
