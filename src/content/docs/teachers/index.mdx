Kampus gives you a course made of nested sections, any activity in any section, exceptions (other dates, attempts or time) for a person or a group in every activity, grades with their full history and progress that is always current. A section leader manages one section the same way a teacher manages a course.

## What you can do

**Designed** means the feature is specified and being built; **Available** gives the version that brought it.
